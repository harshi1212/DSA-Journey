## Tips for This Phase

1. **Solve at least 1 problem daily**
   - 15-30 minutes for easy
   - 30-60 minutes for medium
   - 60+ minutes for hard

2. **Don't skip**
   - Every problem teaches something
   - Each pattern strengthens recognition

3. **Explain to someone**
   - Teach a friend your solution
   - Teaching reveals gaps

4. **Mix difficulties**
   - Don't do all easy
   - Mix easy, medium to stay sharp

5. **Time yourself**
   - Measure improvement
   - First attempt vs. second

6. **Review weekly**
   - Which patterns appear most?
   - Where do you struggle?
   - Revisit those
