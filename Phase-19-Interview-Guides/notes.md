# Phase 19 - Interview Success Guides

## From Good to Great Interview Performance

**Average candidate**: Solves problem, passes tests  
**Great candidate**: Solves optimally, explains thinking, catches edge cases

---

## What This Phase Covers

1. **Edge Case Detection**: What might break your solution?
2. **Common Mistakes**: Patterns that trip up candidates
3. **Communication Checklist**: How to think-aloud effectively
4. **Problem Classification**: Quick pattern recognition
5. **Debugging Strategies**: Fix issues under pressure

---

## Interview Reality

- 45 minutes total
- 15 min: Understanding problem
- 20 min: Coding
- 10 min: Testing & optimization

**Time for edge cases**: 5 min max

---

## Success Metric

✓ Works on all examples  
✓ Handles edge cases  
✓ Optimal complexity  
✓ Clean code  
✓ Good communication  

**5/5 = Strong hire**

---

## Most Common Interview Mistakes

1. **No edge case testing** (40% of failures)
2. **Wrong complexity analysis** (25%)
3. **Off-by-one errors** (20%)
4. **Silent coding** (15%)
